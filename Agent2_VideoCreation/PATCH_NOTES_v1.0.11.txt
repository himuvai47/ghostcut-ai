FacelessRC Agent 2 v1.0.11 — Long-form Diversity / Faceless Pacing

Problem fixed
-------------
Long scripts could be planned as only a handful of huge visual ideas. On a ~4 minute
video this could leave one shot/freeze visible for tens of seconds and encourage repeat
footage, even when hundreds of unused indexed clips were available.

Behavior
--------
- Qwen still decides semantic visual ideas.
- A deterministic pacing pass then splits oversized ideas into roughly 8-second beats,
  with a 10-second visual-segment ceiling and exact contiguous word coverage.
- The existing source-cut rule remains 3-8 seconds.
- Exact product/entity footage is tried first.
- If exact matching cannot cover the beat, Agent 2 relaxes the entity requirement and
  accepts broadly related unused topical B-roll from the curated library.
- If the fast reranker remains too literal, the top unused topical retrieval candidate
  can be accepted as an editorial diversity rescue instead of repeating/freezing.
- Repeats are blocked while at least 12 legal unused clips remain in the library.
- Matched timeline gaps >0.10s now fail Agent 2 QC so Agent 3 cannot silently hide an
  editorial failure with a long freeze.
- Long-form malformed word-ID repair from v1.0.10 remains enabled.

Expected effect on the reported ~4:19 project
----------------------------------------------
A Qwen plan of only 8 large semantic ideas will typically become roughly 30-35 paced
visual beats before matching. With 436 face-safe indexed clips, the matcher should favor
unique related footage and should rarely need repeats.

Cache behavior
--------------
- Whisper alignment is reusable.
- Existing clip embeddings are reusable.
- Visual segments and matching intentionally rebuild once because planner/match policy changed.
