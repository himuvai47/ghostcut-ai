Agent 2 v1.0.16 — Tolerant Editorial Planner Hotfix

Fixes a real v1.0.15 long-form planner failure where Qwen returned a sensible soft label such as "comparison" in preferred_content_types but the old closed enum rejected the whole response.

Changes:
- preferred_shot_types / preferred_content_types are tolerant soft-label arrays at the structured-output boundary.
- Planner normalizes common aliases into Agent 2's supported taxonomy and safely drops unknown soft labels.
- "comparison" normalizes to product_closeup + product_detail rather than crashing.
- Long-form planner gets a 4096-token minimum output budget even if local qwen_num_predict is configured lower.
- Editorial Freedom behavior from v1.0.15 is preserved.
- Matching policy remains unchanged; visual planning refreshes once due to the planner-version bump.
