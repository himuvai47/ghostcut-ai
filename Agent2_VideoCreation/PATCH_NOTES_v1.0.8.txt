FacelessRC Agent 2 v1.0.8 — 3–8 Second Pacing Patch

Changes:
- Normal selected cuts are at least 3.0 seconds and at most 8.0 seconds.
- Clips shorter than 3 seconds are excluded from normal matching.
- Within the same match level, longer relevant clips are preferred.
- Deterministic pacing prevents tiny tail cuts under 3 seconds by rebalancing prior cuts.
- Planner merges sub-3-second visual units with a neighboring unit when possible.
- Multi-entity collective selection only activates when there is enough time for one legal 3-second cut per entity.
- QC validates min/max cut duration.

This changes planning/matching policy, so visual_segments + matching refresh once.
Whisper alignment and cached footage embeddings remain reusable.
