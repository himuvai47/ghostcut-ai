Agent 2 v1.0.14 — Visual Diversity / Near-Duplicate Avoidance

Purpose
- Prevent visually repeated footage that uses different Agent-1 clip IDs.
- Rotate source videos more naturally during long-form faceless edits.
- Preserve v1.0.13 coverage completion, 3-8 second pacing, unused-first matching and face policy.

New behavior
- Penalizes same-source reuse in adjacent/recent beats.
- Strongly deprioritizes clips from already-used nearby source-time regions (default 20s).
- Uses cached clip embeddings + shot/content type to identify near-identical visuals across different source files.
- Adds a mild source-balance penalty so one source video does not dominate when equally relevant alternatives exist.
- Near-duplicates are moved behind diverse alternatives when at least 6 diverse candidates are available.
- Strict/high-specificity named-product beats retain relevance priority.
- Adds visual-diversity diagnostics to timeline.json, qc_report.json and run_summary.json.
- Adds no new model calls and does not rebuild Agent-1 indexes or clip embeddings.

Expected upgrade behavior
- Whisper alignment: reused.
- Visual segments: reused.
- Clip embeddings: reused.
- Matching: refreshed once because MATCH_POLICY_VERSION changes.
